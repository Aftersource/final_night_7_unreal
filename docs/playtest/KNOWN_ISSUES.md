# Final Night 7: known issues

These are things we already know about in the current build. No need to report them, but tell us if one gets in your
way more than we'd guess.

## Known

- **A duel doesn't freeze the world.** By design, everyone should stop and watch a duel. For now the two duellists
  are locked in place, but the world keeps beating around them. The challenge card still says "the world holds its
  breath while you fight"; that's the plan, not yet the behaviour.
- **A lone dead player can't rewind the match yet.** The plan is that a god whose demigods have all fallen can turn
  back time instead of waiting. It isn't built. For now, plead to Death in the WILL (or press the ankh), watch, or
  return to the lobby.
- **The items view is basic.** The ITEMS button on each demigod card shows what it carries in a simple view. A proper
  item card system is still being decided.
- **The own-demigod warning radius is fixed at 300 km.** Dropping a power near your own demigod warns you when it's
  within 300 km, the same at every zoom and for every power, mega or mini. A fix is coming.
- **Performance depends on your PC.** The globe, the board and the living world are heavy. On a slower machine
  expect stutters, especially while panning or zooming. Tell us your graphics card if it's choppy.
- **The build isn't code-signed.** Windows may say "Windows protected your PC": click More info, then Run anyway.

## Unconfirmed

We haven't checked these in the current build, so the guide leaves them out or keeps them vague:

- **Match length.** A match is meant to feel about 7 minutes; the real length depends on the room's settings and on
  how fast the server answers, and it can end early at heat death.
- **SIGN IN WITH GOOGLE.** It shows only when the server offers it. Playing as a guest is the tested path.
- **Watching others' duels.** Watchers should see both lines grow as masked letters. How it looks from the outside
  wasn't re-checked for this guide.
- **Token scenes and world damage.** Floods, wildfires and storms are meant to leave lasting marks at every zoom, and
  some typed actions are meant to play out with little tokens. How much of that shows in this build wasn't checked.
