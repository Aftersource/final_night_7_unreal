# Final Night 7: playtest checklist

Thanks for testing. Each check is "do this → expect that". Tick what works; for anything that doesn't, fill in the
report template at the bottom. Setup and the full guide are in `README_FIRST.txt` (or `README_FIRST.html`), and things
we already know about are in `KNOWN_ISSUES.md`.

Tip: note the time on your clock when something goes wrong. It helps us find it in the log.

## Getting in

- [ ] Launch `FinalNight.exe` → the welcome page
      appears, with no "CAN'T REACH THE GAME" card.
- [ ] Type a name, press **PLAY AS GUEST** → the lobby opens, the narrator welcomes you, and it says "Server online".
- [ ] Press **JOIN THE GAME** → you're at the table, the **HOW TO PLAY** tablet shows (first time only), **GOT IT**
      closes it.
- [ ] Press **HOST A ROOM** → you get a ROOM code; press **CANCEL** → you're back in the house game. Press **LOBBY**
      (or Esc) in a game, then **BACK TO THE GAME** → you're back where you were.
- [ ] Have a friend host, type their code under **JOIN BY CODE**, press **JOIN** → you're in their room.
- [ ] Press **PLAY VS BOTS** → a match of your own starts against AI gods.

## The view

- [ ] Drag the globe → the world spins.
- [ ] Drag the board → it pans, and the spot under your pointer stays under your pointer.
- [ ] Scroll the wheel over empty board, then over a demigod → one zoom level each; over a demigod it zooms onto it.
- [ ] Double-click empty board → zooms in one level. Right-click → zooms out one level.
- [ ] Press **RESET VIEW** (or R, or Home) → the board, camera and panels go back where they were.

## Your demigods

- [ ] Double-click one of your demigods (the piece or its card) → the view centres on it at the same zoom, with a gold
      ring under it.
- [ ] Press **ITEMS** on a demigod card → its items open; press it again or Esc → they close.
- [ ] Drag your demigod onto another spot on the board or the globe → it's summoned there that beat; it may lose 1 to
      2 health.

## Powers

- [ ] Drag a power hex onto the globe → a MEGA (a bolt is spent, the world log says what happened, rivals gain a bolt).
- [ ] Drag a power hex onto the board → a MINI (a spark is spent).
- [ ] Drop a power right on your own demigod → a "Careful: that's YOUR OWN…" warning, and the power stays in your hand.

## The will

- [ ] Click the WILL scroll and start typing → time slows for everyone, and everyone is told it was you.
- [ ] Type an order (for example `Nature, heal my Right Hand`) and press Enter → it's sent, and a ruling or a refusal
      comes back in the world log.
- [ ] Press a hot button (bow, meal or armies) and wait → the line fills the WILL, counts down "goes in 5…", and sends
      itself. (Type in the line instead → the countdown stops.)

## Duels

- [ ] Double-click a rival in reach → the CHALLENGE card shows both names and your odds; Esc → it closes. (A rival
      far away: the view centres on it instead.)
- [ ] Double-click it again and press Enter → the duel starts ("CLASH! Will against will.").
- [ ] Type a move with a verb, a body part and something on the board, press Enter → it lands as a blow, judged at once.
- [ ] Type a move using an item you don't have → it whiffs ("you have no …").

## Falling, the tree, chat and audio

- [ ] With a demigod fallen, open the WILL → the ankh hot button shows; pressing it fills a plea to Death.
- [ ] Press **TREE** → the Tree of Consequence opens; click a node → its details show; Esc → it closes.
- [ ] Click the speech-bubble icon, type a message, press Enter → it appears in the chat; Esc → the chat folds away.
- [ ] Listen through a match → the narrator speaks at the big moments, any key skips, and no crackling in the
      background.
- [ ] Play for 10 minutes → note whether it ever stutters, and what your PC is.
