FINAL NIGHT 7

You are a god at a table of gods. A globe of the real Earth floats over the table, and below it a hex board shows the part of the world you're looking at. Two demigods fight for you down there. You don't press buttons to move them: you write their will, in plain words, and time itself slows while you write. Call down volcanoes. Raise armies. Duel face to face in typed blows. When the Final Night falls, exactly one god is left.

Two demigods each Last god standing wins About 7 minutes a match The world never waits AI gods fill empty seats

Testing for us? See PLAYTEST_CHECKLIST.md and KNOWN_ISSUES.md next to this file.

I.Two steps to the table

1

Unzip

Unzip the Windows build you were sent anywhere you like, for example your Desktop. Inside you'll find FinalNight.exe, an Engine folder and a FinalNight folder.

2

Launch

Double-click FinalNight.exe. The server address is already built into the game, so there's nothing to type in. No access key is needed: sign in as a guest and play.

II.Your first minute

  • Welcome. Pick the name the other gods will know you by (1 to 24 characters), then press PLAY AS GUEST. No email, no password: this computer keeps you signed in. If SIGN IN WITH GOOGLE is offered, that works too.
  • The lobby. A narrator welcomes you. The lobby shows whether the server is online and the house game: the always-on game anyone can drop into, with who's at the table and when the next match starts.
  • Pick your way in.
    JOIN THE GAMETake a seat in the house game. WATCH just watches it.
    PLAY VS BOTSYour own match against AI gods.
    HOST A ROOMYou get a ROOM code to give your friends; press START when everyone is in. CANCEL closes your room and takes you back to the house game (so does BACK TO THE GAME).
    JOIN BY CODEType a friend's room code and press JOIN.
    SETTINGS, DEMIGODS and YOUR GOD in the lobby let you set volumes and options, write your two demigods' personalities, and shape the stone statue of your god at the table.
  • A stone tablet: HOW TO PLAY. It appears the first time you join. GOT IT closes it; the ? button at the top brings it back.
  • Your seat. If a match is already running, you take over a demigod from the next beat on. If a new match is starting, beat 1 is the Final Wish: your first orders get extra power behind them. Think big.
  • The world moves without you. Beats roll on whether you type or not. The AI gods are already scheming. Better start writing.

III.The screen

Top: the headerLOBBY back to the lobby (Esc too) · RESET VIEW the board, camera and panels back where they were (R or Home) · ? how to play · TREE the Tree of Consequence. Also the ROOM, the BEAT (e.g. 12/60), how many are ALIVE, and the ENTROPY meter.
Bottom left: your two demigod cardsA portrait card for each demigod (L and R) with its health. Each has an ITEMS button: click to see what it carries, click again or Esc to close. Your purse sits here too: BOLTS, SPARKS and DEBT (what you owe Time for slowing it).
Bottom right: the WILL scroll"YOUR WILL – click to write". This is where you type. While it's open you get four carved icon hot buttons (section V).
Right side: the power hexes and the pouchCarved wooden hexes in an arc on the table: your powers. The crimson velvet pouch beside them pours them out or puts them away (it opens by itself when you join).
The WORLD logWhat's happening, in plain sentences. The + and − on it fold it open or shut.
ChatA small speech-bubble icon, with a badge when there's something new. Click it to open the chat. Enter sends, Esc leaves.

IV.Controls

The view

Drag the globeSpin the world.
Click the globeDive to that region.
Drag the boardPan. The land under your pointer stays under your pointer.
Mouse wheel (or + −)Zoom one level. Over a demigod, it zooms onto it.
Double-click empty boardZoom in one level.
Right-clickZoom out one level.
R, Home or RESET VIEWBack to the start view.

Your demigods and rivals

Click a demigodSelect it.
Double-click your demigod (piece or card)Centre the view on it at the same zoom, with a gold ring under it.
Double-click a rivalIn reach: the CHALLENGE card with your odds (section VI). Far away: centre the view on it.
Drag your demigod (piece or card) onto the board or the globeSummon it there. It flashes and lands that beat, but summoning is risky: expect to lose 1 to 2 health (it never drops you below 2.5).

Powers

Drag a power onto the globeA MEGA: costs a bolt, hits on map scale, and every other god gains a bolt. Each mega arms your rivals.
Drag a power onto the boardA MINI: costs a spark, hits a small spot.
Drop on your own demigodThe game warns you ("Careful: that's YOUR OWN…") and keeps the power in your hand. Drop it there again to strike anyway.

Keys

EnterOpen the WILL / send it. (Shift+Enter is a new line.)
EscRoll the WILL up (your text stays), close a card, the TREE or the chat, or open and close the lobby.

V.The will

Type what your demigods do, in plain words, then press Enter (or SEND). Start a line with r: for your Right Hand, l: for your Left.

Slowing time. The moment you start typing, time slows for every player, for up to 30 seconds, and everyone is told who did it. Your Enter sends your will and cuts off anyone still typing. Time takes a toll for it: a bolt, plus a little debt.

Name who, where and with what. Say how big. Try:
raise an army of 10,000 fishermen at the harbour of Lagos
arm yourself with an obsidian blade and hunt the nearest rival
Nature, heal my Right Hand
r: march north and topple their shrine

The four hot buttons

☥ AnkhBring back a fallen demigod. Only shows when one has fallen.
🏹 BowArm and hunt a rival.
🍲 MealHeal your most hurt demigod.
⚔ ArmiesRaise an army.

Hover one to read its line. A press fills the WILL with that line and sends it after a 5-second countdown ("goes in 5 · type to make it yours"). Edit the line and it won't go by itself: it's your will then. Press another button to swap the line and restart the count.

Powers, Entropy and fault lines

Powers are separate: typing never casts a power, and a power never carries orders. You start with 1 bolt and 5 sparks. The seven gods are Time, Nature, Consequence, Death, Chance, Memory and Entropy. Every cast pushes the Entropy meter up, and at 100 comes heat death: the Final Night falls at once. Too many megas end the world.

While a mega is in your hand, the planet's real tectonic plate boundaries glow. Megas hit far harder on a fault line, and hardest of all where plates meet; far from any fault they fizzle. Find the lines.

VI.Duels

Double-click a rival in reach. The CHALLENGE card shows who fights whom and your chance to win. Enter or DUEL to fight, Esc or Not now to back off. If the rival is on your board but not in reach, the button reads TELEPORT + DUEL: your demigod jumps beside it first, which can cost 1 to 2 health.

The narrator calls "CLASH! Will against will." You have 30 seconds. Each Enter is a blow: type your move and press Enter to strike. Every line is judged at once by the rules and a d20. Lines with an action verb, a body part, an item you hold or the ground you're on hit harder, so I vault the rock and drive my knee into their ribs beats hit. Repeats, very short lines and gibberish cost you, and so does spamming Enter.

Only what's real. You can use only what your demigod holds or what's there on the board (a tree, a rock, the mud). Name a pistol you don't have and the blow whiffs: "you have no pistol".

Your rival's line shows up masked as they type, with only the newest letters showing: *****p. Read fast and you'll know their move.

VII.Falling, and coming back

Each demigod has 10 health. You're out only when both fall.

Fallen? Plead to Death in your WILL: Death, bring back my demigods, or press the ankh hot button, or PLEAD TO DEATH on the card that tells you they fell. Even a god with both demigods down can plead. Death names a price in sparks; when you can't pay, it sets you a quest. You can also WATCH, or RETURN TO LOBBY.

Curses, seals and mystic quests are in the game too: ask the WILL what does my quest need? and go do it.

VIII.The Tree of Consequence

The game remembers everything, by place and time, from the whole planet down to a single 3-metre hex. Press TREE in the header to see it as a star map: bigger nodes remember more, brighter ones are more recent. Click a node to see what it holds and how one fact travels up into what the Dungeon Master knows. CLOSE or Esc to leave.

IX.Audio

A narrator speaks at the big moments: the welcome, the start of a match, your duel's clash and its end, victory and defeat. Any key skips a line. The crackling in the background sound is fixed. Volumes (Master, Music, Sound effects, Voices) are under SETTINGS in the lobby.

X.Tell us, and troubleshooting

What we want to hear

Everything, in your own words. Blunt is good. "That felt bad" helps us more than polite silence. PLAYTEST_CHECKLIST.md has short checks and a bug template.

  • The first five minutes. Did you get in? Did you know what to do?
  • Your words. Did the world do what you wrote? Did the twists feel fair?
  • The duel. Did it feel like a fight? What should a hit feel like?
  • Controls. Spinning, panning, zooming, dragging. Anything fighting you?
  • Balance. Megas, minis, summoning risk, duel length.
  • The best and worst moment. One of each, please.
CAN'T REACH THE GAME
The game can't get to the server yet. It retries every few seconds by itself.

"Check your internet"

  • The game connects over a normal secure web connection (port 443). If the firewall or antivirus asks, allow FinalNight.
  • A work, school or VPN network might block it. Try another.
  • The server restarts itself now and then. Give it a minute.

"Windows protected your PC"

It's a jam build, so it isn't code-signed. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Slow or choppy?

Performance depends on your PC. Close other heavy programs and tell us your graphics card.

Found a bug? Send us the log: FinalNight\Saved\Logs\FinalNight.log in the game folder.
