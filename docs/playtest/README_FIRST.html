FINAL NIGHT 7

You are a god at a table of gods. Two demigods fight for you across the real Earth. You don't press buttons to move them: you write their will, in plain words, and time itself slows while you write. Call down volcanoes. Curse a rival's grave. Duel face to face in typed blows. When the Final Night falls, exactly one god is left.

Up to 4 gods at the table AI gods fill every empty seat Join any time, even mid-match One always-on game: FINAL7

I.Three steps to the table

1

Unzip

Unzip the build Benito sent you (about 338 MB) anywhere you like, for example your Desktop. Inside you'll find FinalNight.exe, an Engine folder and a FinalNight folder.

2

Drop in your key

Benito sends you an access key privately. It's not in the zip. Make a plain text file with that key on one line, here:

FinalNight\Config\FN7Key.txt

That's the FinalNight folder next to FinalNight.exe. If there's no Config folder in it, create one. The file holds one line: <the key Benito sends you>

3

Launch

Double-click FinalNight.exe. The server address is already built into the game, so there's nothing to type in. Click the velvet bag on the table and you're in.

Notepad trap: Windows hides file extensions, so Notepad can quietly save FN7Key.txt.txt. Turn on View → File name extensions in Explorer and check.
Prefer an environment variable? Instead of the file, run setx FN7_KEY "<the key Benito sends you>" in a Command Prompt, then launch the game. The game reads the file first, then the variable.
Keep the key to yourself. Don't post it or pass it on.

II.Your first minute

  • A dark table under a floating globe. The lobby shows the server's status and the house game FINAL7. Type the name the other gods will see.
  • A crimson velvet bag sits on the table. Click it, or press JOIN THE GAME. The bag pours out your powers: carved wooden hexes in an arc along the table's right side.
  • A stone tablet: HOW TO PLAY. It appears once, the first time you join. The ? button at the top brings it back any time.
  • Your seat. If a match is already running, you take over one of the AI gods' seats, demigods and all, from that beat on. If a new match is starting, beat 1 is the Final Wish: your first orders can be anything, with extra power behind them. Think big.
  • The world moves without you. Beats roll on whether you type or not. AI gods are already scheming. Better start writing.

III.How to play

You have two demigods, your Left Hand and your Right Hand. Each has 10 health and its own place on the planet. You're out only when both fall. Last god standing wins. A match is up to 60 beats, and it can end early: when Entropy hits heat death, the Final Night falls at once.

WRITEType your will in the WILL scroll at the bottom right. Plain words. Time slows for everyone while you write.
CASTDrag a power hex onto the table board for a MINI (1 spark), or onto the globe for a MEGA (1 bolt).
MOVEDrag one of your demigods anywhere on the board or the globe to summon it there. Risky.
FIGHTDouble-click a rival's piece to challenge it to a typed duel. Your words decide the blow.

Controls

Type, then EnterSend your will. Shift+Enter starts a new line.
Start a line with r:That line goes to your Right Hand. Plain lines (or l:) go to your Left Hand.
Drag on the globeTurn the world.
Mouse wheelZoom one level per scroll, centred on the nearest demigod.
Double-click empty mapZoom in one level.
Right-clickZoom out one level (not when you're right on a demigod).
Double-click a demigodCentre the globe and the table on it.
Double-click a rivalChallenge it. A confirm box tells you if your demigod must teleport first and what that costs.
Click the bagPour out your power hexes or put them away. It shows your bolts.
R or Home or RESET VIEWBack to you: the board, the camera and the panels where they were.
Esc or LOBBYOpen or close the lobby.

Bolts and sparks

Your purse starts at 1 bolt and 5 sparks. A MINI costs a spark and hits a few hexes. A MEGA costs a bolt and hits on map scale, and every other god gets a bolt from it, so each mega arms your rivals. The seven gods are Time, Nature, Consequence, Death, Chance, Memory and Entropy. Entropy says yes to everything, and every cast pushes its meter up: freak weather, then broken physics, then riots, then wounds to the gods themselves. At 100 comes heat death and the Final Night falls at once. Too many megas end the world.

IV.The five things to try

⚔

1. Challenge a rival to a typed duel

Double-click a rival's piece. If it's out of reach, your demigod teleports beside it first and loses about 1 to 2 health on the way. Then a carved wooden box rises on the table and opens. Inside, on a hex stage built from the place where you're fighting, two figure-drawing mannequins act out every blow.

You have 30 seconds. Type a move and hit Enter to strike. Every line is a blow, judged at once by the rules and a d20. Lines with an action verb, a body part, an item you hold or the ground you're on hit harder, so I vault the crate and drive my knee into their ribs beats hit. Repeating yourself and spamming both cost you.

Both lines show up masked as you type: each letter flashes for an instant, then hides: *****p. Read fast and you'll know their move. The blue box is your duel. A red box means you're watching someone else's.

▲

2. Cast a mega on a fault line

Pick up a power hex and drag it over the globe. While a mega is in your hand, the planet's real tectonic plate boundaries glow into view. A mega's strength follows its distance to the nearest fault: within about 200 km of a plate boundary it hits about two and a half times as hard, and more than 1,000 km from any fault it's a quarter-strength fizzle. Find the lines. Drop it on them.

⧗

3. Slow time by typing a will

Start typing in the WILL scroll and time slows for every player, for up to 30 seconds, and everyone is told who did it. Your Enter closes the window and cuts off anyone who typed slower. Time takes a toll for it.

Try: raise an army in Lagos and march on the nearest rival
r: arm my demigods with holy spears
heal my demigods

✦

4. Summon your demigod across the world

Grab one of your own demigods and drag it anywhere: onto the board, or onto the other side of the globe. It flashes, whooshes and lands that beat. Summoning often goes wrong (expect to lose 1 to 2 health), but it never drops you below 2.5. Teleport next to a rival, then double-click it and challenge at once.

☠

5. Resurrect, curse and chase quests

Resurrect. Fallen? Plead to Death in your WILL: my demigods come back to life. Even a god with both demigods down can plead. Death keeps a price list in sparks and years of life, and when you can't pay, it sets you an errand.

Curse. curse <rival>'s Right Hand with withering (no healing reaches it), or seal a grave with no resurrection for <rival>. Also: bind, silence, hex. A curse costs sparks, and it's rolled: on a natural 1 it turns on you.

Quests. Every curse comes with a way to break it: a mystic quest along the lines of "the wings of a butterfly that crossed a strait, burned at dawn on the shrine of the one who cursed you". Ask what does my quest need? and go do it.

VI.Troubleshooting

CAN'T REACH THE GAME
If this stone card sits over the table, the game can't get to the server yet. It retries every few seconds by itself.

"...has no access key"

The key didn't load, or it's wrong. Check:

  • The file is exactly FinalNight\Config\FN7Key.txt, inside the FinalNight folder next to the exe.
  • It isn't secretly FN7Key.txt.txt.
  • It holds just the key, copied exactly (no quotes needed).
  • Restart the game after fixing it. Still no luck? Ask Benito for the key again.

"Check your internet"

  • The game connects out over a normal secure web connection (port 443). If Windows Firewall or your antivirus asks, allow FinalNight.
  • A work, school or VPN network might block it. Try another network.
  • The server restarts itself between matches. Give it a minute.

"Windows protected your PC"

It's a jam build, so it isn't code-signed. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Found a bug? Send Benito the log: FinalNight\Saved\Logs\FinalNight.log in the game folder.
