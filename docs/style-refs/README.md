# Benito's house style references

Benito chose these 15 pictures as the look for Final Night 7's generated images (portraits, statues, event images, the end-of-match storyboard).

The look: a dark-fantasy world at night, in saturated cobalt blue and violet, under starry skies with swirling nebulae and a thin crescent moon. Warm orange torch and fire light is the only contrast. It looks like an early-2000s PlayStation 2-era fantasy cutscene: low-poly models, crunchy low-resolution textures, heavy contrast, simple baked lighting. Dreamy and eerie.

The text style he used (scripts/round-render.mjs, DEFAULT_STYLE):

> a dark-fantasy world at night: a burning stone keep of crumbling walls, torch-lit corridors and Gothic archways, standing at the edge of a misty pine forest under a vast starry sky with swirling purple and blue nebulae and a thin crescent moon. Deep saturated cobalt blue and violet everywhere, warm orange torch and fire light as the only contrast, glowing sparks and fireflies. Rendered to look like an early-2000s PlayStation 2 era fantasy game cutscene: low-poly models, crunchy over-sharpened low-resolution textures, heavy contrast, slightly jagged edges, simple baked lighting, dreamy and eerie

With the reference images (images/edits): "Match the reference images exactly in art style, rendering, colour palette, lighting and level of detail; this is a new shot in that same world, not a copy of them."

Always: "No words or letters in the picture, no gore, no real people."
